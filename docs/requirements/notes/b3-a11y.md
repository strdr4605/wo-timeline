# Unit b3-a11y: builder notes

Builder, 2026-11-10. Lead: t38. Item: I5 (accessibility and keyboard navigation).

## What changed

- **Bar** (`work-order-bar.*`): the bar is focusable (`tabindex 0`, `role="group"`). Its name is
  "name, status, dates", the content of the tooltip. Enter or Space opens the three-dot menu and
  moves the focus to Edit. ArrowUp, ArrowDown, Home and End move between the items, with
  wrap-around. Tab also moves between the items. Tab out of the bar closes the menu. Escape
  closes the menu and gives the focus back to the bar.
- The three-dot button has `aria-label="Actions for <name>"` and `aria-haspopup="menu"`. It has
  `tabindex -1`: it is transparent at rest, and the bar gives the same action. The closed menu is
  `inert`, so its transparent items are not in the Tab order.
- A menu item click focuses the bar before it emits. The drawer then gives the focus back to the
  bar on close.
- Focus rings show only on `:focus-visible` and use `$color-border-focus`: the bar (2px, offset
  1px), the menu items, the timescale options, the drawer buttons. The bar shows its three-dot
  button while it has the keyboard focus.
- **Timescale chip** (`timescale-chip.*`): the button is named "Timescale: <value>", has
  `aria-controls` to the listbox, and the listbox is named "Timescale". Enter, Space, ArrowDown or
  ArrowUp from the keyboard open the menu with the focus on the selected option. The arrows, Home
  and End move. Enter or Space selects and gives the focus back to the button. Escape closes and
  gives the focus back. A pointer click does not move the focus (`event.detail` is not 0), so the
  View Selection state is unchanged.
- **Drawer** (`work-order-drawer.*`): `aria-modal="true"`, `aria-describedby` to the subtitle.
  The Cancel button is named "Cancel and close the drawer". Open moves the focus to the name
  field. Close gives the focus back to the element that had the focus at open. Tab and Shift+Tab
  wrap inside the drawer. Tab after a click on the empty panel brings the focus back to the
  first control. The Escape order of D9 and the Tab order of D10 are unchanged.
- **Today button** (`app.html`): `aria-label="Today: scroll the timeline to today"`.
- `src/app/keyboard.ts`: the arrow-key rule that the bar menu and the timescale menu share.
- `@axe-core/playwright` ^4.14.0 added as a dev dependency. It installed cleanly. The dev server
  did not need a restart: only the e2e tests import it.

## Parity mode

- Parity mode does not move the focus to the name field on open. The design shows the open
  drawer with no focus ring ("Placeholder and Defaults"). The first try did move it, and D11
  failed (border of the focus state at rest). That is the reason for the exception.
- No new element is visible at rest. All new rings are `:focus-visible` only.

## What still differs and why

- **axe color-contrast, serious, accepted (lead decision).** Three design colours are under
  4.5:1. They are kept for pixel parity:
  1. The chip label `#687196` on `#f5f6fa` (4.42:1).
  2. The Complete green `#08a268` on `#e1ffcc` (3.03:1), on the bar badge and on the drawer
     status pill.
  3. The Open cyan `#00b0bf` on `#e4fdff` (2.48:1), on the bar badge.

  The spec accepts only these nodes (`DESIGN_CONTRAST_NODES`), only for the rule
  `color-contrast`. Any other serious or critical violation fails the test.
- Empty grid cells are not keyboard reachable (create via keyboard). Not in the I5 scope.
